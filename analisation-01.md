# Rinsly — Pricing & Positioning Analysis

*Prepared from the live site content (Payload seed = what renders on rinsly.com) plus current Dutch-market research, July 2026. All Rinsly figures are read directly off the site; all prices below are **ex. 21% VAT** unless noted. I'm not a financial or legal advisor — SLA and compliance wording flagged at the end warrants professional review before publishing.*

---

## 1. What Rinsly actually offers (read off the site)

**Product:** Custom website build + fully-managed hosting/maintenance, on a headless stack — **Payload CMS + Next.js/React on Cloudflare** (D1/R2, single Worker). Positioned as "websites that work, fully managed" for solo founders and startups. One point of contact; stack is flexible ("prefer a different technology? we adapt").

**Recurring plans** (hosting included in every plan; annual = 11× monthly, i.e. **1 month free ≈ 8.3% off**):

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | For whom | Included | Excluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Care** | €49 | €539 | "Sites we built" | Cloudflare hosting, SSL & uptime monitoring, security & CMS updates, weekly backups | Changes, continuous development |
| **Volledig beheerd** *(Recommended)* | €99 | €1.089 | "Everything taken care of" | All of Care + **up to 1 hr/mo changes** + response ≤2 business days + dedicated contact | — |
| **Partner** | €199 | €2.189 | "Sites that need to grow" | All of Fully managed + **up to 4 hrs/mo development** + proactive SEO/perf + quarterly review & roadmap + response ≤1 business day | — |
| **Op maat** | On request | — | "Larger organisations" | All of Partner + SLA with guaranteed response times + more dev hours + security & compliance agreements | — |

**Contract & ownership (from FAQ, not the pricing block):**
- **No lock-in** — ongoing, monthly cancellable, 1-month notice.
- **Customer owns the site and all data**, always.
- **Build (one-off) is NOT on the pricing page.** It appears only in an FAQ: "depends on size/complexity, we make a tailored quote," and "the build cost can be spread over the first year." No number, not even a "from."

The build being invisible and the SLA/compliance signals being hidden behind "on request" are the two structural issues that drive most of what follows.

## 2. The Dutch market (named competitors, advertised prices)

Three distinct product classes get compared under the same headline numbers. Keeping them separate is the whole game.

**A. All-in "website-abonnement" (build financed inside the monthly) — closest headline analogue, but a different product:**
- **Webtify** — Business €50/mo **+ €250 setup**; Business Plus €150/mo + €350 setup. WordPress; 12–24 hrs/yr changes. ([webtify.nl](https://webtify.nl/website-abonnement/))
- **Froseo** — Start €69/mo, Plus €149/mo (+€199 setup, waived on annual). **Froseo retains framework ownership; annual contracts lock for a year.** ([froseo.nl](https://froseo.nl/website-abonnement/))
- **Webarctic** €45/mo, **Webframer** €59/mo, **Vwebdesign** €39/mo — WordPress-template abonnementen, mostly no visible setup fee (cost recovered via lock-in). ([webarctic.nl](https://webarctic.nl/website-abonnement-kleine-ondernemers/), [webframer.nl](https://webframer.nl/maandelijkse-website/), [vwebdesign.nl](https://www.vwebdesign.nl/abonnement/website-130))

⚠️ **Not apples-to-apples:** these are overwhelmingly **WordPress on templates, on shared hosting**, and the "free" build is paid for through **setup fees and/or multi-year or annual lock-in**, sometimes with the **agency keeping ownership of the framework** (Froseo). Rinsly is a **custom headless build on premium infra, fully owned by the client, cancellable monthly** — a materially higher-value product at a similar or slightly higher headline.

**B. Pure maintenance / "WordPress-onderhoud" subscriptions (compare to Care):**
- **WJonderhoud** €12,50/mo · **KeurigOnline** from €20/mo · **Hardam** €35 / €59/mo · **Webbeheer Nederland** €42/mo · **Antum** €69/mo. ([wjonderhoud.nl](https://wjonderhoud.nl/), [keurigonline.nl](https://www.keurigonline.nl/wordpress-onderhoud), [hardamontwerp.nl](https://www.hardamontwerp.nl/website-onderhoud/), [webbeheernederland.nl](https://www.webbeheernederland.nl/website-onderhoud-abonnement), [antum.nl](https://www.antum.nl/website-onderhoud/))
- Market consensus for basic maintenance: **€30–150/mo**; mid-size sites €150–300/mo. ([nolimitdesign.nl](https://nolimitdesign.nl/inspiratiehub/website-onderhoud-bespaar-kosten-met-slim-onderhoud/), [pcpatrol.nl](https://pcpatrol.nl/wat-kost-wordpress-onderhoud-in-2026-eerlijke-prijsvergelijking-en-rekenvoorbeeld-voor-mkb-en-webbureaus/))

**C. Managed hosting only (the floor):**
- **TransIP** Managed WordPress from **€3,99/mo**; **Savvii** from **€29/mo**. ([transip.nl](https://www.transip.nl/webhosting/managed-wordpress/), [wpmaintenance.com](https://www.wpmaintenance.com/woocommerce-hosting-providers/))

**D. Custom build (the one-off Rinsly hides):**
- MKB/business site **€2.000–7.500**; true maatwerk **€5.000–40.000+**. Agency hourly **€75–125**; freelance **€60–120**, zzp average **€73/hr**. ([searchlab.nl](https://searchlab.nl/blog/wat-kost-een-website-in-2026), [onlinelabs.nl](https://www.onlinelabs.nl/blog/wat-kost-een-website-laten-bouwen), [next-win.nl](https://next-win.nl/kenniscentrum/webdesigner-uurtarief/), [bieb.knab.nl](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/wat-verdient-een-web-developer-zzp-bekijk-uurtarief-en-winst))
- The nearest stack-peer, **OnlineLabs**, sells "WordPress CMS + Next.js frontend, 95+ PageSpeed" — confirming the headless angle is a real, marketable differentiator, not a niche. ([onlinelabs.nl](https://www.onlinelabs.nl/blog/wat-kost-een-website-laten-bouwen))

*VAT caveat: consumer-facing abonnement pages often quote incl. or unspecified VAT; Rinsly correctly states ex-VAT. Some headline gaps narrow or widen once normalised — treat competitor numbers as ±21%.*

## 3. Is Rinsly low, mid, or high? (tier by tier)

| Tier | Headline vs market | Value delivered | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Care €49** | **Mid** for maintenance (€30–150 band; above TransIP/Savvii hosting, above WJonderhoud/KeurigOnline, below Antum €69) | **High** — premium Cloudflare infra, full monitoring/backups, no lock-in, full ownership | **Mid price / high value → underpriced.** Well above bare hosting is justified. |
| **Volledig beheerd €99** | **High headline** vs all-in abonnementen (€39–69 typical; = Webtify Business €50 + setup) | **High** — custom headless, 1 hr/mo, owned, cancellable | **Mid-to-high price / high value.** Only "expensive" against template-WordPress it doesn't compete with. |
| **Partner €199** | **Mid-high** (Froseo Plus €149, Webtify Plus €150; below mid-size-site €150–300 band) | **High but mispriced** — see below | **Under-monetised.** |
| **Op maat (on request)** | Can't be benchmarked — that's the problem | Unknown to the buyer | **Opaque.** |

**The core pricing finding — the ladder prices *hours*, not *value*, and prices those hours cheaply.** Strip out Care's €49 base and the increments read as raw labour:
- Volledig beheerd: +€50/mo for "up to 1 hr" → ~€50/hr
- Partner: +€100/mo for "up to 4 hrs" → **~€33–50/hr effective**

Both sit **below the €73 zzp average and far below the €75–125 agency rate.** For solo founders this is attractive and fine. But it (a) leaves money on the table, and (b) signals "cheap freelance labour" to exactly the larger buyers Rinsly wants next — who read price as a proxy for reliability.

## 4. Does the structure serve the "grow into larger companies" goal?

**Entry point: yes.** €49 Care is a sensible, honest on-ramp for a founder who just wants their site kept alive, with a clear upgrade path (Care visibly greys out "changes" and "doorontwikkeling").

**Steps between tiers: uneven and hour-anchored.** €49 → €99 → €199 → ??? The first three steps are fine; then it **falls off a cliff into opaque "on request."** A larger buyer scanning the page sees a **visible ceiling of €199** and infers a small shop. That is the single biggest thing working *against* the growth ambition.

**Signals larger buyers care about are hidden.** SLA, guaranteed response times, security/compliance, dedicated hours — everything an MKB/enterprise procurement checklist wants — is bundled into "Op maat: on request." **Staging environment and uptime guarantee aren't mentioned at all.** Larger buyers don't ask; they filter out vendors who don't show these.

**Model mismatch.** The ladder is built around *hours of work* (a small-client mental model). Larger clients buy *business outcomes and risk reduction*: uptime, response SLAs, roadmap, compliance posture. The current framing tops out speaking the wrong language.

**Build invisibility hurts twice.** With no build number on the page, (1) the recurring price looks high next to abonnementen that appear to include a "free" build, and (2) there's no anchor establishing that a €2.500–7.500 custom asset justifies the monthly. The FAQ's "spread over the first year" is a genuinely strong startup-friendly offer that's currently buried.

## 5. Recommendations (with numbers, each traced to a benchmark)

**Guiding move: keep the founder-friendly entry, but raise the visible ceiling and re-anchor on value, not hours.**

### Recommended pricing — current vs proposed (ex. VAT)

| Tier | Current | Proposed | Rationale (benchmark) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Care** | €49/mo | **€49/mo** (keep) | Correct entry anchor. Sits mid-band (€30–150 maintenance) with premium-infra justification. Don't touch it — it's the on-ramp. |
| **Volledig beheerd** *(Recommended)* | €99/mo | **€99/mo** (keep) | Best-positioned tier. Above template abonnementen (€39–69) but defensible on ownership + no lock-in + headless. Keep as the highlighted default. |
| **Partner** | €199/mo | **€249/mo** + add **staging environment** | 4 hrs/mo dev at market (€75–125/hr) is worth €300–500 alone; €249 still undercuts that while moving off €33/hr optics. Re-frame as "growth partner," lead with roadmap/SEO outcomes, keep hours as a fair-use cap not the headline. |
| **Op maat** → rename **Scale / Enterprise** | On request | **"vanaf €499/mo"** + list the signals | Convert opaque "on request" into a **priced-from** tier that visibly names: guaranteed SLA (e.g. response ≤4 business hrs), 99.9% uptime target, staging, security/compliance baseline, DPA/verwerkersovereenkomst, dedicated hours. Gives larger buyers a reason to self-qualify. Floor tied to mid-size-site retainers (€150–300+) plus real SLA overhead. |
| **One-off build** | Hidden in FAQ | **Surface: "Eenmalige bouw vanaf €2.500 (excl. btw) — spreiden over het eerste jaar mogelijk"** | Market MKB build €2.000–7.500. Anchors the value of the recurring fee and makes the startup-friendly financing a selling point instead of a buried footnote. |

**Resulting ladder: €49 → €99 → €249 → from €499.** Cleaner spacing (~2× steps), a visible high anchor, and no cliff into the unknown.

**Annual-discount strategy:** keep **1 month free (~8.3%)** on Care/Volledig beheerd — accessible, matches market. On **Partner/Scale, offer 2 months free (~16.7%)** on annual prepay to reward commitment *without* reintroducing lock-in (monthly-cancellable stays the differentiator vs Froseo's annual lock). This nudges the higher tiers toward predictable revenue while preserving the "no lock-in" story.

**Positioning copy, not just price:** on the pricing intro, make the three real differentiators explicit next to the numbers — **(1) you own the site & data, (2) monthly cancellable, (3) custom headless on Cloudflare, not a WordPress template.** These are precisely where competitors are weakest (setup fees, annual lock, retained framework ownership, shared hosting) and where Rinsly's higher headline becomes obviously justified.

### What would sharpen this
I'm benchmarking to the market, not to Rinsly's own data. The recommendation would tighten with: current **tier mix & conversion** (is anyone actually buying Partner? does "Op maat" convert or die?), **churn** by tier, **average build quote** and how often the spread-over-year option is taken, and **support-hour utilisation** (are "up to 1/4 hrs" mostly unused? — if so, raising Partner is nearly free margin).

### Flags for professional review
- **SLA / "gegarandeerde reactietijden" / 99.9% uptime** — once published these are contractual commitments; have wording (and any uptime guarantee on Cloudflare's SLA) reviewed legally, and back them with credits/remedies you can actually honour.
- **"Security & compliance agreements"** and the **DPA/verwerkersovereenkomst** (you already have a `rinsly-offerte` skill that generates one) — get the compliance claims and GDPR wording reviewed before advertising them to larger/enterprise buyers.
